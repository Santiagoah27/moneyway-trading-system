## Methods investigated

| Method and original source | Event, inputs and causal confirmation | Suitability and adaptation for US100 H4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dow peak and trough analysis, Charles Dow as developed by William Peter Hamilton. [Hamilton, The Stock Market Barometer](https://archive.org/details/stockmarketbarom00hami) · [StockCharts explanation](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/market-analysis/dow-theory) | A succession of rising or falling peaks and troughs describes a trend. A turn needs subsequent observed price action. The concept supplies neither OHLC leg counts nor an H4 confirmation clock. The classical framework also considers averages and corroboration. | Useful conceptual separation of a level from a trend. MoneyWay retains its own closed-bar pivot definition and requires explicit structure evidence. A complete Dow Theory implementation on one instrument would require additional inputs and rules. |
| Confirmed and projected ZigZag pivots, TradingView. [Official calculation and limitations](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000591664-zigzag-indicator/) | Neighboring bars confirm an extreme only after the right leg exists. The reversal deviation, leg count, alternation and replacement affect what is retained. The visible chart can draw a point at its older extreme and may revise the rightmost leg. | Reuse the timing distinction and keep provisional points separate. V2 retains strict MoneyWay comparisons, keeps both sides of a double pivot and preserves the confirmation timestamp. TradingView's percentage, equality asymmetry and replacement behavior are outside the experiment. |
| Directional change, Edward P. K. Tsang, Shuai Ma and V. L. Raju Chinthalapati. [Nowcasting directional change in high frequency FX markets](https://eprints.goldsmiths.ac.uk/37843/1/Intelligent%20Systems%20in%20Accounting%20%20Finance%20and%20Management%20-%202024%20-%20Tsang%20-%20Nowcasting%20directional%20change%20in%20high%20frequency.pdf) | A reversal from a running extreme is confirmed by a later observation crossing a specified threshold. The extreme and confirming event are distinct. An implementation uses ordered observations and a threshold. The paper's nowcast is a separate predictive task and uses historical distributions. | A future MoneyWay experiment could use closes to process a threshold causally or use ordered finer data after admission. H4 OHLC alone cannot resolve a same-bar reversal sequence. Threshold, sampling and initial direction would add new assumptions. This approach was deferred. |
| Computational chart patterns, Andrew W. Lo, Harry Mamaysky and Jiang Wang. [Foundations of Technical Analysis](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf) | Local extrema come from kernel-smoothed series and then satisfy geometric pattern criteria. A causal H4 adaptation would require a bounded window, an explicit bandwidth and a rule freezing availability only after the supporting observations. | Illustrates a reproducible way to formalize patterns. Retrospective smoothing cannot be read as an as-of pivot without an explicit causal adaptation. Bandwidth and endpoint choices would add sensitivity that this sample cannot compare. |
| David H. Bailey, Jonathan M. Borwein, Marcos López de Prado and Qiji Jim Zhu. [The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://www.risk.net/journal-of-computational-finance/2471206/the-probability-of-backtest-overfitting) · [Authors' manuscript](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4w1110bb) | This is an evaluation method, using in-sample and out-of-sample rankings across a matrix of strategy histories. ## Hypothesis selection and implementation

| Hypothesis | Causal and concept fit | Data, interpretability and engineering tradeoff | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Expand original history | Strong if exact H4 source, time mapping and availability are established. Preserves V1 and adds earlier neighborhoods. | Easiest to explain and implement with the existing loader. Sensitive to the requested start and gaps. Synthetic extended history proves the software can classify HH/HL and LL/LH. The actual earliest retained row prevents this sample from estimating the improvement. | Next source experiment. |
| B. Separate confirmed reference, trend and current interaction | Strong. Older levels can remain observable without a confirmed trend. The latest classified event and structure keep separate evidence and times. | Works on the same packet. High interpretability and reuse. Keeps legacy profiles and horizons without new numeric tuning. Zero-improvement results remain visible. | **Implemented V2**. |
| C. Causal directional change or different pivot geometry | Possible with explicit later confirmation. Price thresholds or smoother-based turning points are hypotheses beyond the audited H4 selection. | A threshold adds sensitivity, initialization choices and more tests. Intrabar ordering requires finer evidence. The single exposed case cannot select a threshold. | Deferred with no fitting on June 23. |

V2 uses the V1 strict 1/1, 2/2 and 3/3 neighborhoods and its 1- and 2-close delayed-fakeout horizons. These six engineering configurations are retained for sensitivity, with no preferred profile or mentor attribution. Right confirmation delays are 4, 8 and 12 hours in a contiguous H4 window. A partially observed strict neighborhood stays provisional, prints the original candles it actually used, and carries a null confirmation timestamp. A closed source candle can supply both extrema without imposing intrabar order.

The V2 report retains every visible original H4 candle, the confirmed-reference neighborhoods, the last closed candle's level and wick evidence, and the original times of qualified events. It can report a provisional or ambiguous structure, a confirmed trend, an unavailable context, or a confirmed reference without a new event. It recognizes strict V1 breakout and immediate or delayed rejection evidence. A delayed rejection is dated at its own confirming close. Wick coverage is retained as geometry. The source leaves the selection and precedence of full strategic Wickfill unresolved. A simultaneous two-sided penetration or multiple qualified events at the latest close is ambiguous.

An experimental V2 completion requires a single qualified event at the latest H4 close and matching confirmed structure direction, a same-direction take at or after preparation, and the existing causal M5/M1 binding and geometry checks. H4 evidence cannot be applied retroactively to an earlier take. Completion stays documentary. Counts of associations per profile are separate from distinct M1 events, grouped by direction and confirming timestamp. This comparison derives M5/M1 evidence from the same existing synchronized replay and keeps each underlying evidence ID.

## Reproduction and next technical step

This opt-in Worker mode accepts the exact pinned June 23 Development input. It rejects altered packets, registration inputs and protected-session fixtures before replay. Source and report hashes support reproducibility. The frozen protocol, its evaluation state, version 1.1.0, canonical replay and active rules retain their authority.

```powershell
$source = (Resolve-Path 'src/frontend/moneyway-web/src/research/context/data/june23-context.json').Path
$run = Join-Path $env:TEMP ('moneyway-h4-causal-v2-' + [guid]::NewGuid())
dotnet run --no-launch-profile --project src/backend/MoneyWay.Worker -- nasdaq-session-liquidity --input $source --output (Join-Path $run 'v2') --compare-h4-causal-context-v2 --cutoff-utc 2026-06-23T16:30:00Z
dotnet run --no-launch-profile --project src/backend/MoneyWay.Worker -- nasdaq-session-liquidity --input $source --output (Join-Path $run 'v1') --compare-end-to-end --cutoff-utc 2026-06-23T16:30:00Z
```

The files are `v2/nasdaq-h4-causal-context-v2.json` and `v1/nasdaq-end-to-end-research.json`. To verify the preparation snapshot directly, rerun V2 in a new directory with `--cutoff-utc 2026-06-23T13:00:00Z`. Integration tests match that snapshot to the 16:30 report and verify that 12:59 has no preparation evidence. Unit tests append and mutate future H4 candles, including a candle open at the cutoff, and compare the complete serialized snapshot. Cases include strict equality, double extrema, both directions, documented closures and unexplained gaps.

**Decision.** Expand original H4 history as the next controlled experiment. Acquire and pin an earlier OANDA Demo US100 H4 export with exact native times, mapping evidence, gaps and acquisition provenance. Compare nested causal prefixes at a fixed preparation cutoff with all six configurations and record stability and any boundary effects. Use fresh exposed Development data if acquisition would overlap a protected partition. A single session offers insufficient evidence to infer accuracy, economic performance, generalization or optimal thresholds. Multiple profiles are sensitivity checks and make retrospective winner selection especially misleading.
