## Architecture and concrete evidence

- [CsvMarketDataImporter](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Infrastructure/MarketData/Csv/CsvMarketDataImporter.cs) exposes Import(TextReader) and ImportFile(string), returning an immutable homogeneous [CandleSeries](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Domain/MarketData/CandleSeries.cs).
- [RunMultiTimeframeReplayUseCase](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/MarketData/Replay/RunMultiTimeframeReplayUseCase.cs) supplies synchronized bounded frames to a callback. It supports optional canonical price observations without resampling candles.
- [GenerateMultiTimeframeStrategyBacktestRunUseCase](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/Backtesting/GenerateMultiTimeframeStrategyBacktestRunUseCase.cs) owns canonical evaluation, workflow and lifecycle progression. Its typed-input overloads snapshot inputObservations with ToArray before replay. Its only dynamic producer concerns lifecycle evidence, not binding human strategy observations or historical artifacts.
- [GenerateCanonicalMultiTimeframeBacktestUseCase](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/Backtesting/GenerateCanonicalMultiTimeframeBacktestUseCase.cs) exposes the strategy diagnostic report; it has no factual trade result slot or per-frame factual consumer hook.
- Downstream human evidence requires prior canonical facts: [NasdaqPreEntryEligibilityRuleFact](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/Strategies/Nasdaq/ReplayInputs/NasdaqPreEntryEligibilityRuleFact.cs) has an internal constructor and must be obtained from canonical history, not fabricated. Observed entry/SL bind that fact; TP/risk bind canonical SL; documented exit binds the exact assembled snapshot. A completed run retains compact market observations, not the full bounded contexts needed by the source selectors and contact collector.
- Historical unit fixtures demonstrate these primitives with synthetic records and obtain prerequisite facts through staged runs. They are not an imported real mentor dataset or proof that all raw mentor records can already bind in one canonical run.

## Canonical CSV header

```csv
provider_id,symbol,timeframe_amount,timeframe_unit,open_time_utc,close_time_utc,open,high,low,close,volume
```

Use (4, Hour), (1, Hour), (5, Minute), (1, Minute) for the four files. Normalize actual source timestamps to UTC with documented source timezone; do not reinterpret unknown broker timestamps. A later reviewer annotation does not establish contemporaneous availability. Original retained contemporaneous records may substantiate availability even if imported later.
