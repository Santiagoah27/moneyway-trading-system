The 4H bootstrap has a confirmed boundary. The mentor uses no fixed candle/day/session count or numeric lookback; instead, the visual process begins from an evident High/Low that originated the current directional leg. That anchor concept is confirmed, while selecting the first anchor and terminating the historical scan over arbitrary raw candles remain `human_validation_required`. Once valid structural references exist, the latest strict formally closed body break supplies inherited bullish/bearish direction and operational history compresses to the resulting HH+HL or LL+LH pair without deleting older audit evidence. Wick-only penetration, equality and current candle colors alone do not establish direction. Weekly open, previous-day extrema and Asia/London levels remain separate context references rather than mandatory H4 bootstrap seeds.

## Canonical gated workflow

1. From 08:00 `America/Bogota`, after the relevant 4H candle closes, review its context and permitted direction.
2. Mark Asia/London extrema and relevant 1H/4H structural liquidity references; these levels do not seed 4H structure.
3. From 08:30, the first valid liquidity take establishes the effective Step 3 for the scenario that may proceed; require alignment with the Step-1 4H permitted direction before enabling any 5M setup.
4. On 5M, require Structural Change `OR` IFVG, whichever occurs first.
5. Separately require the directional 5M FVG confirmation produced after the Step-4 trigger.
6. On 1M, require a countertrend pullback toward/into that FVG and structural realignment before entry eligibility.

For trading day `D`, preparation is complete only when Step 1 (review of the formally closed 4H candle, structural context, Breakout/Wickfill/Fakeout classification and bullish/bearish bias) and Step 2 (Asia/London highs and lows plus applicable relevant 1H/4H structural levels) have both actually been completed for that same session. Completion is eligible in `[08:00:00, 08:30:00)` `America/Bogota`; reaching 08:00 or merely having candles does not prove completion. At exactly 08:30, Step 3 can begin only if preparation was already complete. Missing that deadline means no trade for `D`, with no late recovery; preparation expires with the morning session and must be renewed for the next trading day. The [canonical replay input contract](../../decisions/0006-define-nasdaq-preparation-replay-input-contract.md) is specified, but its runtime observation and `NQ-TIME-003` evaluator are not implemented.

The operational entry-acquisition window is `[08:30, 11:00)` in `America/Bogota`. All pre-entry gates must complete before 11:00. At 11:00, any unfinished setup expires; do not continue into the mentor's "zona muerta," chase price or carry the incomplete setup into a later day. This cutoff does not define forced closure of a conceptual trade entered before 11:00.

After 08:30 there is no separate interval in which an effective opposite-liquidity consumption occurs "before Step 3": that first valid take is the observed Step 3. Taking a relevant Low can orient a possible buy progression and taking a relevant High can orient a possible sell progression, but only when that orientation matches the Step-1 4H permitted direction. Otherwise Step 4 is not enabled, the originally imagined scenario remains discarded, and price is not chased. Step-3 geometry remains the audited `exceeds relevant High` or `goes below relevant Low`; conversational use of "touch/take" does not establish equality-only activation.

On the relevant 1H or 4H structure, a candidate low becomes a validated HL only after the impulse originating from it produces a formally closed candle body above the prior HH. On the bearish side, after a prior LL and cessation of new lows, the first bullish candle that begins upward displacement starts the correction. A distinct preceding LL-producing candle, including an exact doji at that extreme, remains in the previous bearish impulse and outside the new correction and its geometry. If that candle also prints a lower `Low`, the new extreme first updates the correction-origin floor and its bullish close then starts the correction from that floor; the new price extreme is not automatically a validated structural LL. Once active, every in-range bullish, bearish or exact-doji candle remains in that same correction turn despite internal oscillation or consolidation. A strict `Low < current correction-origin floor` ordinarily resets the correction, updates the floor and discards the candidate LH when that candle does not also confirm the candidate with `Close < prior LL`. A bullish reset candle is excluded from the old turn and included as the first member of the new bearish correction; a neutral or bearish reset candle starts no new correction. A formally closed `Close > prior validated LH` invalidates bearish structure; equality and wick-only penetration do not. The invalidation candle is excluded from the destroyed turn and candidate geometry and belongs to the new bullish impulse context. Its candidate high otherwise remains provisional until a formally closed candle has `Close < prior LL`, when the correction ends and the new LL and preceding highest candidate high become validated from that causal `AsOfUtc`. Within the same recognized bearish correction, a higher candidate replaces a lower candidate and superseded intermediate highs do not survive as LH candidates. Historical replay cannot use either candidate as validated before its confirming close. For an already selected LH turn, the structural coordinate is its highest candle-body edge: the maximum `Open` or `Close` among member candles, independent of candle color; a higher wick does not redefine it. Protection anchors remain separate: buy uses the relevant HL `Low`/lowest wick and sell uses the relevant LH `High`/highest wick, while the actual Stop Loss offset below/above that anchor remains unresolved. The causal break candle is excluded from the old correction membership and retrospective candidate scanning; it belongs to the impulsive expansion, and its close confirms the new HH/LL, ends the correction and validates the preceding candidate. In the single-expansive-candle case, that candle's `Open` supplies the structural HL/LH origin coordinate and its `Close` supplies causal validation without making it a member of the old correction turn or requiring an intrabar path. This case-specific rule does not replace multi-candle selected-turn body-edge rules. The bearish terminal mechanics are confirmed as the causal mirror of the bullish side, but both windows remain partial because near-doji thresholds, historical scan termination and deterministic initial-anchor selection remain unresolved. For bullish candidate HL, the prior HH remains the upper boundary; after HH production stops, the first bearish candle starts the correction. If the HH-producing candle is distinct, it remains in the previous bullish impulse and outside the new correction; an exact-doji HH candle before that bearish body is likewise excluded. Every intervening bullish or exact-doji waiting candle also stays outside until the first `Close < Open`, even if it extends the ceiling with a new High or remains contained in the HH exhaustion area. Bearish-structure waiting candles are excluded until the first `Close > Open`; a strict new Low updates the floor without starting the correction. After ResetAndAwaitCorrectionStart, the reset candle and subsequent waiting candles remain outside. None of these pre-start candles contributes Open, Close, High or Low to the future turn's StructuralPrice or ProtectionAnchor. Once active, every in-range bearish, bullish or exact-doji candle remains in that same correction turn despite internal oscillation or consolidation, until a causal confirmation or terminal/reset event occurs; color alternation does not create parallel HL candidates. A strict `High > current correction-origin ceiling` ordinarily resets the correction, updates the price ceiling and discards its candidate without automatically validating structural HH when that candle does not also confirm with `Close > prior HH`. If that same closed candle is bearish, it is excluded from the old turn and included as the first member of the new bullish-correction turn; if neutral or bullish, no new correction starts. A formally closed `Close < prior validated HL` invalidates bullish structure; equality and wick-only penetration do not. Its candle is excluded from the destroyed turn and candidate geometry and starts bearish impulse evidence without automatically validating structural LL. The deepest low/base remains the candidate and replaces a shallower low; one- or two-candle pauses inside the confirming impulse do not create another HL. Within the selected turn, use the lowest body `Open`/`Close`, not an isolated wick. Exact equal lowest body coordinates form one HL/LH structural price level; no candle identity, first/last, timestamp or sequence tie-break is required. Multiple exact-equal bodies may support that one horizontal level without defining a numeric zone width. A lower/HL turn uses its minimum body edge as StructuralPrice and minimum Low as ProtectionAnchor; an upper/LH turn uses its maximum body edge as StructuralPrice and maximum High as ProtectionAnchor. Different candles may supply those extrema without creating two structural points. Near-equal merging tolerance and the executable Stop Loss offset remain unresolved.

After an aligned valid liquidity take, wait for Step 4 while the setup remains inside the operational window and no audited cancellation has occurred. A subsequent same-side take or farther extreme before Step 4 keeps the same single setup at Step 3 and updates its active 5M structural reference; it does not create a parallel setup or advance Step 4. Historical references remain auditable. The primary opposite-side target candidates are the Step-2 Asia/London session extrema: London Low or Asia Low for a sell-oriented setup, and London High or Asia High for a buy-oriented setup. When two future-valid session levels differ, the immediate target is the first relevant level price encounters in the expected direction; neither session has fixed priority. When both prices coincide, they form one shared target rather than two sequential targets, and the reviewed scenario treats that shared level as final Take Profit without a separate intermediate Break-Even target. If no session target remains future-valid before the operational scenario, a sell may fall back to relevant unmitigated support at a validated 1H/4H HL or LL, or Previous Day Low; a buy may fall back to relevant unmitigated resistance at a validated 1H/4H LH or HH, or Previous Day High. In reviewed paths with distinct relevant structural levels, 1H is the first objective or management level and the farther, structurally stronger 4H level is the final or extended objective. This observed hierarchy is not a universal formula. Candidate-HL selection and its selected-turn lowest-body anchor are partially defined; confirmed bearish candidate-LH identity and its selected-turn highest-body-edge coordinate still require unresolved correction-edge and other structural-point semantics before target selection can be deterministic. Mitigation and PDH/PDL ranking remain non-deterministic. This fallback does not rescue an originally imagined scenario whose opposite liquidity is consumed after 08:30. Exact selected-target contact remains sufficient, including wick contact, without body close, candle-close confirmation or tolerance. The remaining active-bullish-correction collision is resolved. The bearish mirror collision is also resolved. If the same formally closed candle has both `Low < current correction-origin floor` and `Close > prior validated LH`, invalidation dominates. Current canonical gating uses only previously established prerequisites, so a future mapping of both gates to the same replay frame remains an explicit unresolved discrepancy.

Opening/quotation gaps have no special structural role. The missing interval is not interpolated and an opening gap is not an FVG/IFVG or a Step-4/Step-5/Step-6 signal.

## Critical open variables

Human review confirms both same-candle confirmation-plus-origin-extension events. Bullish `Close > prior HH` with `High > origin ceiling` validates the old HL and rolls the pair to new HH + validated HL; bearish `Close < prior LL` with `Low < origin floor` validates the old LH and rolls to new LL + validated LH. The confirming candle is excluded from old-turn geometry, but starts the next correction as its sole initial member when its exact body is corrective; otherwise the valid side is `AwaitingCorrectionStart` with an empty next turn. Valid bullish `HL < HH` and bearish `LL < LH` ordering makes simultaneous strict candidate confirmation and opposite-point invalidation impossible. Ordinary non-confirming reset and reset/invalidation rules remain separate. The scoped `StructuralCandidateTurnBoundary` specification is ready for deterministic implementation with supplied valid references and an active candidate turn; full raw-history detection is still partial.

The prior structural HH/LL is the candidate-validation reference; the current correction-origin ceiling/floor is a separate reset extreme. Within an active candidate turn, equality or wick-only penetration of the prior HH/LL stays non-confirming and included, with OHLC eligible for selected-turn geometry, unless an independent reset or invalidation event applies. A formally closed strict body break validates the candidate and excludes its confirming candle from the old candidate turn. Prior-HH/LL inclusivity is resolved; near-doji treatment, initial-anchor selection, historical scan termination and other structural coordinates remain partial.
