## Bullish correction collision clarification

The structural semantics documented by `NQ-H4-001` and consumed conceptually when `NQ-LIQ-002` references causally validated structure include one resolved collision boundary. When the same formally closed candle has both `High > current correction-origin ceiling` and `Close < prior validated HL`, both strict observations are true and `BULLISH_RESET_INVALIDATION_COLLISION_PRECEDENCE = CONFIRMED_INVALIDATION_DOMINATES`. The bullish candidate is destroyed; the candle is excluded from the previous bullish turn and candidate geometry and belongs to the new bearish impulse context. Its `High` remains a relevant upper price extreme/bearish-origin reference and possible wick-based protection reference for that new context, but is not automatically a validated LH, structural High or executable Stop Loss. The result becomes knowable only at the candle's causal close, never rewrites earlier snapshots and requires no synthetic intrabar chronology. This clarification creates no new `RuleId` and is not a mapping to `NQ-LIQ-003`, `NQ-M5-001`, IFVG/FVG confirmation, entry gating or same-frame workflow propagation.

## Bearish correction mirror clarification

Direct human evidence confirms `BEARISH_RESET_INVALIDATION_SYMMETRY = CONFIRMED_MIRROR`. In an active bearish correction, in-range color alternation, internal consolidation and exact doji remain in one turn. A strict `Low < current correction-origin floor` resets the correction, updates the floor and discards the candidate LH; the same candle starts the new bearish correction only when `Close > Open`. A neutral or bearish reset candle starts no new correction. A formally closed `Close > prior validated LH` invalidates bearish structure, while equality and wick-only penetration do not. The invalidation candle is excluded from the destroyed turn and candidate geometry and belongs to the new bullish impulse context.

When the same closed candle has both the strict new Low and prior-LH body-close invalidation, invalidation dominates. The candidate is destroyed and no new bearish correction starts. The candle's `Low` remains a lower price extreme, bullish-origin reference and wick-based protection evidence, but is not automatically a validated HL, Structural Low or executable Stop Loss. These facts become knowable at the causal close and never mutate prior snapshots or imply intrabar chronology.

For the reviewed bearish collision only, the mentor identifies the lower-wick event as liquidity take and the body close above prior LH as structural change. That evidence is context-specific. It creates no new `RuleId`, does not map every collision to `NQ-LIQ-003` or `NQ-M5-001`, and does not bypass selected-liquidity, 4H alignment, timeframe or other workflow prerequisites. The canonical progression fold currently uses prerequisites established before the current frame; a future human-approved case in which both gates map to one causal frame therefore has an unresolved same-frame gating discrepancy. No prerequisite graph or runtime behavior changes here.

An exact doji with `Close == Open` is directionally neutral and starts neither correction; if it prints a new `High` or `Low`, the applicable ceiling/floor still updates while correction remains not started. Neither same-candle origin-extreme update bypasses the independent formally closed body rule for structural HH/LL validation, and no future data or intrabar sequence supplies direction. A causal closed `Close < prior LL` ends it and validates the new LL and preceding highest candidate LH from that `AsOfUtc`. Within that recognized correction, a higher candidate replaces a lower one. For the selected LH turn, the structural target/comparison coordinate is its highest `Open`/`Close` body edge; candle color is non-normative and a higher wick does not redefine it. In both directions, the confirming break candle is excluded from correction membership/scanning and belongs to the impulsive expansion. In the single-expansive-candle case, its `Open` supplies the structural HL/LH origin coordinate and its `Close` supplies causal validation; this does not replace multi-candle selected-turn rules. The bullish window's in-range continuity is confirmed. Opening/quotation gaps have no special structural role: the Open does not start, reset, invalidate or fragment structure by itself; exact body direction, strict origin extremes and formally closed Close rules remain governing, no missing path is interpolated, and the discontinuity is not FVG/IFVG.
