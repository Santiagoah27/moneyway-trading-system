# ADR 0025: Define Nasdaq Demo v0 human-assisted evidence contract

## Status

Accepted. **DEMO V0 integration contract, not new mentor strategy semantics.** This ADR specifies future typed inputs and their consumers; it implements no types, evaluators, orchestration or trade simulation. Rule definition states and replay capabilities remain unchanged. Human assistance does not establish an autonomous algorithm or authorize an order.

## Date

2026-10-03.

## Context

The demo-first audit at `f4754f4` found a usable canonical replay pipeline, but no complete Nasdaq session evidence contract or historical trade simulation. Current Nasdaq has 32 RuleIds, 14 Required, four registered evaluators (`NQ-TIME-003`, `NQ-LIQ-001`, `NQ-TIME-001`, `NQ-TIME-002`), ten Required evaluator gaps and no full Required registration. The capability catalog declares zero HumanOnly entries; this ADR does not change that fact.

The [Nasdaq specification](../strategies/nasdaq/strategy-specification.md) establishes the concepts, workflow and limits. [ADR 0003](0003-define-canonical-replay-prerequisite-gating.md) separates raw facts from progression. [ADR 0005](0005-transport-strategy-owned-evidence-to-replay-lifecycle-policies.md) supplies immutable scoped evidence conventions. [ADR 0006](0006-define-nasdaq-preparation-replay-input-contract.md) specifies timely preparation. [ADR 0007](0007-define-nasdaq-human-origin-vertex-replay-input.md), [ADR 0008](0008-define-nasdaq-human-origin-vertex-conflict-policy.md) and [ADR 0009](0009-define-nasdaq-rebuilt-candidate-vertex-replay-input.md) establish typed membership, exact event identity, compatible support, conflicts and causal visibility. Their specific H4 episode contracts are not generic session-context seeds.

## Decision

### Existing conventions and scope

Reuse `IStrategyReplayInputObservation` for evaluator-driving observations: immutable strategy/version, provider, symbol, UTC `ObservedAtUtc` and nonempty `SourceReference`. Use `StrategyReplayContext.InputObservations`, exact reference resolution and strategy-owned selectors. Evaluators must not perform hidden file/network/database lookups. Preserve the canonical Application entrypoint, workflow, lifecycle, outcome and diagnostics; no second replay or diagnostic framework.

No new generic evidence framework or common C# envelope is needed now. Each proposed semantic type adds its own event fields to the existing interface. Provenance may additionally carry reviewer/source identity, exact file/video/message location and external mentor reference. A provenance reference must resolve to retained immutable supporting material, not an unexplained free-form assertion. Compatible selection retains every supporting observation and its provenance, rather than only a representative reference.

`HumanRuleOverride`, `Dictionary<RuleId, bool>`, caller-assigned rule results and arbitrary RuleId payloads are forbidden. Inputs state market/review facts; consumers calculate their own results. Future autonomous adapters may replace human selection without changing those facts. No input changes `IsRequired`, definition status or capability metadata.

### Identity and causal availability

- `NasdaqDemoSessionIdentity`: exact strategy ID/version, provider, symbol and trading date `D` in `America/Bogota`. This is an analysis scope; existing H4 episodes retain their cross-session identities and lifetimes.
- Exact candle reference: the enclosing series identity, timeframe amount/unit and series-unique `OpenTimeUtc`, resolved against its actual `CloseTimeUtc` and OHLC. A multi-member structural reference retains all selected candle identities, its High/Low and HH/HL/LL/LH role, and the source validating event where applicable. Derived body coordinates and wick protection remain distinct. A non-candle level needs an exact retained external level/event reference and price; an unexplained decimal is insufficient.
- `NasdaqDemoSetupIdentity`: session identity plus the initiating valid liquidity-take reference, taken level identity/side and observable event identity. Quote events use timestamp and authoritative source sequence when present; closed-OHLC events use the exact candle and its observable interval, not an invented intrabar instant. Ambiguous events cannot produce a unique setup identity. Farther same-side updates before Step 4 retain the initiating setup identity and append their event/reference lineage; they do not silently start another setup.
- Session-level preparation facts do not need a setup that does not yet exist. Downstream facts bind to the setup and exact predecessor event/FVG identity. Candidate observations can be recorded before a prerequisite is established; this never grants workflow eligibility.
- `EffectiveAtUtc` is the market event or reviewed-context time. `ObservedAtUtc` is when that semantic assertion became source-observable, not merely when its underlying candle occurred. Both are UTC. Require `EffectiveAtUtc <= ObservedAtUtc`; every necessary candle must have actually closed and every required price event must be observable before consumption.
- At frame `T`, consumption requires `ObservedAtUtc <= StrategyReplayContext.AsOfUtc = T`, exact identity, resolved observable sources and applicable workflow/lifecycle eligibility. Availability is never source priority. A late assertion affects only current/later frames; no earlier results, deadlines or signals are rewritten.
- Import time may be later than authentic historical availability when contemporaneous source material substantiates it. Record import/review time separately. Later analyst creation of a new assertion cannot claim the old candle time as its availability. Unknown availability is not synthesized.
- Auxiliary observations currently do not create replay boundaries by themselves. Consume at the first existing canonical boundary at/after availability; never shift timestamps or fabricate a boundary. Missing event resolution must remain explicit.

### Selection and runtime mapping for these new contracts

Select visible observations for the exact semantic slot: session preparation-context/reference selection, setup first trigger, setup selected FVG and its quality review, selected pullback, selected realignment, or operation parameter/event. Compare the complete typed semantic payload, including exact identities and selections; provenance/record ordering alone does not distinguish compatible facts. Unordered member collections compare as exact sets. Different prices, directions, selected members, first-trigger claims or review decisions in the same slot conflict, even if some later calculation happens to agree. Distinct sequential management events are separate slots; contradictory values for one event conflict.

`Missing`, `Unique` and `Conflict` are selection outcomes, not new rule-result values. `ObservedAtUtc` controls visibility, not semantic equality: compatible reviews of the same effective fact may have different availability times and provenance. Unique compatible support allows reference validation and rule evaluation; it is not automatically `Passed`. Preserve all supporting records and all conflicting alternatives. There is no latest/earliest-wins, vote, source priority, union/intersection, tolerance or automatic supersession. Explicit correction/revision authority remains outside Demo v0; a visible conflict blocks that dependency from then onward.

The future adapters use the following mappings, defined only for the new Demo contracts:

- **H**: a due human review/selection has no usable assertion -> `human_validation_required`.
- **W/H**: while awaiting a market pattern or predecessor -> `waiting`; an observable candidate requiring a missing human review -> `human_validation_required`. Absence is not evidence that the market pattern failed.
- **D/H**: missing numeric/source/account data -> `data_unavailable`; unresolved human selection or basis -> `human_validation_required`.
- Every conflicting valid driving assertion -> `human_validation_required`; downstream processing cannot choose one. Malformed identity/timestamp input is rejected by validation, not converted to a positive rule result. Unresolvable referenced market data -> `data_unavailable`.

These mappings do not resolve missing-evidence policies in older H4 reconstruction contracts. Existing TIME/LIQ evaluators keep their exact status behavior: notably a missed preparation deadline is not replaced with H. Missing evaluator registration still produces incomplete coverage; documentation does not fill it. Missing entry/management records are operation-result limitations, not automatic rule failures or `no_trade` verdicts.

Price ownership depends on the **source structural model as well as class and timeframe**; a class-only switch would lose existing exceptions.

The [NQ-LIQ-002 definition](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/StrategyDefinitions/Nasdaq/MoneyWayNasdaqStrategyDefinition.cs), [liquidity specification](../strategies/nasdaq/strategy-specification.md#10-liquidity) and [selected-turn fallback boundary](../strategies/nasdaq/strategy-specification.md#171-structural-fallback-evidence-boundary) establish selected-turn HL/LH body coordinates for reviewed **1H/4H** references. Arbitrary support/resistance zones without a recognized class and exact documented coordinate are likewise not admitted. Generic Upper/Lower calculator sides and wick `ProtectionAnchor` values are not additional liquidity classes. No new class is introduced.

**B — exact binding and causal validation.** Every admitted reference retains the enclosing Demo session (strategy ID/exact version, provider, symbol, Bogota trading date), exact 1H or 4H timeframe amount/unit, structural class, matching side (HH/LH High; HL/LL Low), source model/ownership basis and explicit decimal `StructuralPrice`. Retain a nonempty exact source candle/reference set, resolved against series-unique `OpenTimeUtc`, actual `CloseTimeUtc` and OHLC, and immutable human provenance identifying the selected point and coordinate. One reference can have one or multiple members; a selection can separately contain multiple references. Do not flatten a multi-member point into a wick candle, merge references merely because prices coincide, choose a nearest candle, or invent member identities.

When the source model has selected body members, preserve every exact member. An `008` reference instead preserves its actual episode/validating event and human price evidence: that contract explicitly supplies no body-coordinate owner. Model-specific event identities and guards from the linked ADRs remain mandatory; session identity cannot replace completion, turn or collision identity. Where establishment depends on a closed-body break, retain its exact confirming event and prior structural reference; confirmation alone is not a coordinate formula. Human-reviewed initial anchors retain their actual reviewed context/anchor provenance rather than a fabricated completion or historical scan. A naked `price = 21500`, a provisional extreme or a wick label without this basis is unusable.

The ordinary selected-turn rows include resolved H4 `009` rebuilt HL/LH only under [ADR 0009](0009-define-nasdaq-rebuilt-candidate-vertex-replay-input.md): retain the actual migration episode, exact final members, known protection equality and subsequent completion. Direct candidate completion retains its existing authoritative candidate geometry and formation sources; it does not create a new rebuilt membership. These H4 constraints and the `007`/`008` branches do not define 1H lifecycle models or relax their own original contracts.

`EffectiveAtUtc` records the reviewed selection/context time, no earlier than observability of all required source facts (actual closed members and applicable validation/turn/completion events). `ObservedAtUtc` records when that assertion became observable and must satisfy `EffectiveAtUtc <= ObservedAtUtc <= AsOfUtc`. Resolve only exact currently available source data. A later review or later-closing member never backfills earlier frames. Retrospective mentor annotations remain comparison-only metadata as specified below; importing authentic contemporaneous evidence later does not change its substantiated historical availability.

**Formula-backed validation.** Preserve the explicit price already required by the selection contract for audit/display, and derive the authoritative value from the exact selected source using only the applicable existing formula. Require exact decimal equality: no epsilon, rounding repair, tolerance or silently corrected input. An inconsistent supplied price is invalid evidence and cannot become a usable `Unique` fact or pass a rule. Missing source data remains unavailable; it is not permission to trust the typed price. [StructuralTurnBodyCoordinateCalculator](../../src/backend/MoneyWay.Application/MarketData/Candles/StructuralTurnBodyCoordinateCalculator.cs) provides ordinary lower/upper aggregation, but does not select members, validate a point, or decide that a model applies. A known formula-backed model cannot opt into human ownership to evade equality checks.

**Human-documented validation.** For admitted unresolved-coordinate models, the human owns the exact decimal, including its retained source/provenance. Validate identity, class/side, admitted model, exact source existence, event basis, timeframe, price presence and causal availability. Do not validate that price against `High`, `Low`, `Open` or `Close` without an authoritative model-specific relation; do not assign a fictitious candle as its body owner. This supports assisted canonical replay, not autonomous coordinate discovery. Ordinary HL/LH and scoped post-completion HH/LL keep their deterministic ownership even though a human selects members.

There is **no universal structural High -> `Candle.High` or structural Low -> `Candle.Low` rule**. `StructuralPrice` supplies the selected liquidity coordinate; `ProtectionAnchor` is separate wick geometry under its existing model and is not substituted for that price or made an executable SL. Body and wick extrema may have different owners. This clarification authorizes no wick-based structural-liquidity substitute.

Selection compares the complete exact reference set, including class, side, model/ownership, numeric coordinate, source members/events and effective fact. Unordered input/member order is non-semantic. Different valid selected sets, classes, human coordinates or memberships conflict even if derived prices coincide; preserve all alternatives/support. Invalid formula-price mismatches are validation failures, not competing legitimate coordinate authority. Missing never auto-passes; Conflict has no winner, voting, merge or supersession. Preserve the existing H/D/C mappings above and do not add a rule-result value.

Downstream NQ-LIQ-003 needs the validated, causally available exact price and High/Low side; the numeric strict comparison is independent of price ownership. Retain ownership and all evidence for diagnostics. **High requires `price > StructuralPrice`; Low requires `price < StructuralPrice`**. Equality, touch-only activation, penetration thresholds and tolerances remain excluded. Timing, H4 alignment, prerequisites and lifecycle eligibility remain unchanged.

**Feature 3 is specification-UNBLOCKED** for the admitted rows: class/model/timeframe, source binding, explicit price ownership, formula equality where applicable and causal availability are now defined. The stopped next feature remains only the typed NQ-LIQ-002 structural-liquidity selection evidence and its Missing/Unique/Conflict selector through canonical replay. Excluded provisional/previous-day/arbitrary references must not be added to that feature. No evaluator, NQ-LIQ-003 implementation, discovery algorithm, strategy-status promotion or capability registration follows from this documentation.

### Demo human-assisted NQ-LIQ-003 take evidence clarification

Audited baseline: `e78dd27`, 32 Nasdaq rules, 14 Required, six registered evaluators, eight Required evaluator gaps, full registration false. The attempted autonomous strict-take primitive stopped without changes. **Autonomous NQ-LIQ-003 detection remains `BlockedByUnresolvedSpecification` as a specification limitation**, independently of future human-assisted coverage. Authorized observed-price interpretation, detection resolution, intrabar/closed-candle attribution and eligibility-straddling intervals remain unresolved. This label is not a new capability declaration: current NQ-LIQ-003 evaluator coverage remains `NotImplemented`, and its rule definition remains `Confirmed`. Earlier four-evaluator counts in this ADR describe the original audit.

For Demo v0, permit a typed positive **human-observed take fact** when authentic evidence identifies the exact event, price and time. The human identifies the event; MoneyWay validates known identities, arithmetic, ordering and visibility. This replaces the earlier proposed unconditional deterministic `NasdaqLiquidityTakeFact` route for Demo v0. It is an evidence bridge, not a new trading rule, automatic discovery, first-take search, signal or permission to trade.

#### Exact reference binding and minimum payload

The conceptual `NasdaqHumanLiquidityTakeObservation` uses the existing `IStrategyReplayInputObservation` envelope and `NasdaqDemoSessionIdentity`. Retain an immutable exact upstream reference binding, semantic High/Low side, exact reference price, exact human-observed market price, `ReferenceEligibleAtUtc`, `EffectiveAtUtc`, `ObservedAtUtc`, exact source-event identity and nonempty `SourceReference`. The source reference must resolve to retained supporting material identifying the price, event time and authentic availability; it is not an unexplained assertion. No `Passed` flag, arbitrary RuleId, trade direction or generic override belongs in this input.

Keep the two reference families distinct; current types provide no common complete reference identity:

- **Session:** exact session/provider/symbol/version/day and one named endpoint `AsiaHigh`, `AsiaLow`, `LondonHigh` or `LondonLow`. Resolve that endpoint against the actual available output of the existing `NasdaqSessionLiquidityCalculator` and its exact closed 1H session sources. The carried price must equal that endpoint exactly; its name fixes High/Low side. Retain the endpoint selection and eligibility provenance. `NasdaqSessionLiquidityLevels` alone lacks provider/version/source identity and is insufficient as an unscoped key. Naming the endpoint records the human's selected reference; it does not infer relevance, rank sessions, merge equal-price endpoint identities or change NQ-LIQ-001.
- **Structural:** exact session and the exact NQ-LIQ-002 selected fact (reference set, selection effective time), with one exact member `NasdaqStructuralLiquidityReference`. Resolve through the existing Missing/Unique/Conflict selector; the member must belong to that usable Unique set with exact model, role, timeframe, source/member/episode identities and price. A decimal or role alone is not an identity. Preserve all selection/reference support and `FormulaBacked`/`HumanDocumented` ownership. Consume the already-established `StructuralPrice`; no second geometry calculation or reinterpretation is authorized.

Identity is semantic, not collection position or an audit-string tie-break. Different reference families or endpoints remain distinct even at equal prices. Required source facts and compatible upstream selection must remain observable at consumption; a visible upstream conflict blocks the binding. No fabricated H4 episode, provisional point or previous-day fallback enters through this contract.

#### Exact event time, availability and eligibility

`EffectiveAtUtc` is the exact UTC market time documented for the human-identified take event, **not** the replay evaluation time, review/import time or a fabricated candle-extreme time. Admit either an exact retained canonical `HistoricalMarketPriceObservation` (provider/symbol, timestamp, actual price, source kind/resolution and authoritative sequence when supplied), or an authentic mentor/source event record explicitly documenting exact UTC market time, observed price and stable event identity. A local source timestamp needs a documented timezone conversion; a video playback offset or approximate chart annotation alone does not establish exact market time. The canonical price contract already exists but is neutral: human review supplies this event's take interpretation; admitting its identity does not define autonomous bid/ask/trade semantics or introduce a feed.

`ObservedAtUtc` is when this take assertion, including necessary reviewed reference/event facts, genuinely became source-observable. Require UTC and `EffectiveAtUtc <= ObservedAtUtc <= context.AsOfUtc`. Supporting price events must be observable and supporting candles closed by consumption. Import time may be later than authentic availability only when retained contemporaneous records substantiate that distinction. No wall-clock inference, backdating or earlier-frame rewrite is allowed.

`ReferenceEligibleAtUtc` is a **source-backed availability boundary for this exact selected reference**, not a caller-chosen bypass of workflow. It cannot precede the reference's effective selection, required source availability or the availability of the upstream human assertion(s) relied upon. For session endpoints, completed session-source coverage and documented selection must already exist; for structural members, the exact selected fact and relied-upon usable support must already exist. Check **`ReferenceEligibleAtUtc <= TakeEffectiveAtUtc`**, with `TakeEffectiveAtUtc = EffectiveAtUtc`. Equality at this temporal boundary is permitted; price equality remains invalid. If the record cannot substantiate eligibility by the claimed take time, it is unusable. Evidence available only after an old event cannot establish that reference's eligibility for the old event merely by being visible now.

The observation/selector owns validation of this reference-event pair and causal visibility. The future canonical evaluator/workflow/lifecycle retains preparation, post-08:30/before-11:00 timing, H4 alignment, existing prerequisites and active-setup rules; `ReferenceEligibleAtUtc` does not encode or override them. Candidate input presence never grants progression or same-frame prerequisite propagation.

A closed candle may be retained as **supporting provenance only**, using the existing exact series/timeframe/OpenTime/CloseTime/OHLC binding and visibility checks. Its range cannot alone establish an exact take event, an authorized detection timeframe or order across a boundary inside the interval. An interval beginning before reference eligibility and ending after it needs an independent source-backed exact event/time resolving ordering. Do not derive that instant from High, Low, Close, candle color or interpolation. No 1M/5M/1H/4H take-detection timeframe is selected here.

Retrospective mentor/analyst annotations without substantiated historical availability remain **comparison/debug metadata only**, outside evaluator-driving input. A later-published video does not prove an assertion was available at the recorded market time. Authentic contemporaneous records imported later may drive only frames at/after their substantiated availability, retaining original evidence and import/review provenance. Unknown event time or availability cannot be synthesized into a driving fact.

#### Strict validation, selection and runtime family

For High, **`ObservedPrice > ReferencePrice`**; for Low, **`ObservedPrice < ReferencePrice`**. Use exact existing decimal representation with no threshold, epsilon, tick count, buffer or tolerance. The equation is identical for session, FormulaBacked and HumanDocumented references. Human review cannot override it.

Typed construction owns positive-fact arithmetic and timestamp validation: reject equality, wrong-side penetration, malformed identities or impossible timestamp order as invalid input. An identity/price mismatch with actual upstream evidence, unproven eligibility or unsupported event-time claim is unusable at binding/selection validation. None is negative evidence that NQ-LIQ-003 failed, and none becomes `Failed` or `Passed`. Unresolvable named market sources remain a `data_unavailable` dependency under this ADR; unresolved human basis/order remains `human_validation_required`.

For the next observation/selector feature, the semantic slot is **one human-identified initiating take for the exact upstream selected fact/reference in the exact session**. A caller cannot partition competing initiating-event claims by using different event IDs. Compare complete reference binding, side/price, eligibility boundary, event identity, effective time and observed price. Different claims about the initiating event conflict; different claimed eligibility boundaries for the same upstream selected fact also conflict, rather than creating independent slots. Subsequent same-side takes are later lifecycle events outside this selector's initiating-take slot; this clarification neither ranks multiple references nor discovers the session's first take.

- **Missing:** no usable human assertion -> `human_validation_required` for the due Demo human-adapter dependency. Absence proves neither a take nor its failure. This supersedes the earlier deterministic-row `waiting without a take` assumption for this assisted path; normal prerequisite waiting remains owned by canonical workflow. Preserve a distinct unavailable-source diagnostic when a named source cannot resolve.
- **Unique:** compatible complete facts retain **all** supporting observations and provenance, including differing authentic availability times. Expose the exact reference, price and effective event time; this permits owning-rule validation, not automatic `Passed`, Step 3 or a signal.
- **Conflict:** materially different visible claims for the same slot -> `human_validation_required`, preserving all alternatives. Availability/provenance alone does not distinguish the semantic fact. No latest/earliest winner, voting, supersession, union or silent event-time correction.

Downstream integration can bind the future 5M trigger to this exact initiating take and its truthful `EffectiveAtUtc`; chronological `take -> then 5M` checks still require observable source order and established canonical prerequisites. Unknown equal-time order does not manufacture precedence. No 5M trigger geometry or same-frame bypass is defined here.

**The typed human take observation plus Missing/Unique/Conflict selector is specification-UNBLOCKED** under these bounds. Authentic eligible references and exact contemporaneous event evidence remain dataset requirements. This documentation implements no observation, selector, evaluator, detector, capability registration or downstream lifecycle. The next feature is only that typed evidence/selector boundary; autonomous detection remains blocked and capability counts remain 32/14/6/8/full=false.

### Entry, SL, TP and historical-result ownership

`HistoricalObservedEntry` is an observed/documented execution parameter, never a strategy-derived entry algorithm. Bind it to setup, direction, exact execution event/time, actual price and source execution/mentor record; retain quantity and unit if known. Entry is not candle Open/Close or a next-open fill unless the historical record explicitly documents that execution. It cannot establish a signal, reverse a cancellation or satisfy earlier gates. A documented mentor entry without a causally eligible MoneyWay signal is a comparison mismatch, not a MoneyWay trade. The maximum-loss amount is human/account input; quantity is documented input, not MoneyWay-calculated size. The future deterministic boundary checks complete compatible units/identity and `reported maximum loss / documented account basis <= 0.01`. It does not certify an undocumented loss formula or choose a balance/equity convention. An unsupported account basis or loss calculation requires human validation; missing amounts require data unavailable. This is explicitly a reviewed risk assessment, not independent position-sizing proof. A later actual fill can support a post-entry audit but cannot backfill the earlier risk gate.

For each observed management event retain operation identity, exact source event/order identity, event kind, effective time, availability, prior/new stop or exit price, quantity/unit for partials, reason and supporting market reference. Different events append chronologically; competing descriptions of the same event conflict. Actual execution history is not automatically strategy compliance.

Risk input validation requires a positive account basis and nonnegative reported loss; quantity, when supplied as traded exposure, must be positive with an explicit unit. Invalid numeric inputs cannot pass the ratio check.

The source-backed BE concept is favorable first encountered distinct Asia/London target touch, independent of timeframe, followed by SL to entry.
